【Official Game Rules】

  • Game Flow: The game proceeds in 4 steps: Your Base ➡ AI's Base ➡ Your Boost ➡ AI's Boost. Each player gets an independent, fresh 13-card Face Deck for their own Boost phase.
  • Phase 1: Base Phase (54-Card Deck / No Loss): Build your score foundation safely.
    • If the first card is 7 or lower, succeed by drawing equal/higher than that card; if the first card is 8 or higher, succeed by drawing equal/lower than that card.
    • Draw cards continuously. In "UP" mode, draw equal/higher. In "DOWN" mode, draw equal/lower to succeed.
    • Drawing 【A】 or 【7】 triggers Mode Reverse (UP ⇄ DOWN), guarantees safety, and grants a free pass for the next card. JOKER resets the target and awards +10 points.
    • ※ J=11, Q=12, K=13, JOKER=10. Failing a match merely ends your phase; your points are NEVER confiscated.
    • Starting Bonus: If your 1st card is 8 or higher (8 to K), the phase automatically starts in "DOWN" mode for your advantage.
    • 【Streak Bonus: Suit Shield】: If you draw 7 or more cards in this phase (including the 1st card), you earn a Suit Shield (Barrier) that protects you from busting in Phase 2! One Shield is added for every multiple of 7 cards drawn (7, 14, 21...).
  • Phase 2: Boost Phase (Face Card Only / Risk of Reset): Amplify your points in a high-stakes thrill ride.
    • The deck changes to 13 cards containing only Face cards (J, Q, K) and 1 JOKER.
    • Draw cards. If the card is of the same suit or a stronger suit (♠＞♥＞♦＞♣) than the previous one, you succeed!
    • Multiplier gains: J = +0x (keeps streak) / Q = +1x / K = +2x / JOKER = +3x.
    • Press 【STAND】 to lock in your current multiplier safely.
    • 【Multiplier Reset】: If you fail a suit match, your multiplier is reset back to 1.0x and your turn ends. However, the Base Points you earned in Phase 1 are safely protected! (If you have a Shield, it will automatically break to prevent the reset and let you continue).
  • Core System: Hold Slot (Absolute Defense)
    • You can use the Hold function once per phase, independently.
    • HOLD (Save): Move your current card into the slot and deal a new card to the field.
    • USE HOLD (Activate): Overwrite the dangerous field card with your held card.
    • Activation Effect: Completely ignores rank constraints (in Phase 1) or suit constraints (in Phase 2) for an absolute 100% success, resetting the field target to maximum safety for the next turn.
